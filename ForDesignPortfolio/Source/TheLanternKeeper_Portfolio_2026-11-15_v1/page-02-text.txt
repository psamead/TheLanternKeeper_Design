THE GIRL WHO STILL REMEMBERS
Maela, the keeper's granddaughter, is the story's point of view. Her
restrained proportions and clear outline support a quiet folk-fantasy
tone. The warm lantern remains distinct from her yellow coat.
FROM CONCEPT TO PLAYABLE CHARACTER
The early concept carries the lamp in her left hand; the game-
facing version uses the right. Separated body parts support cut-out
movement and a clear lantern-raising action.
